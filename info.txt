~/commonplace is a folder on my desktop. It is also this website.

I drop things into the folder and they are automatically posted here. A website without an interface. A new type of posting gesture.

Inspired by "commonplace books," the notebooks writers and thinkers once used to keep track of quotes, scraps of thought, doodles, ephemera. Commonplace books were a precursor to modern curation and digital scrapbook culture.
