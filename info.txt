~/commonplace is a folder on my desktop. It is also this website.

I was interested in the idea of a website with zero interface, published locally from a single folder on my desktop. 

I drop things into the folder and they are automatically posted here. Style and text is controlled for a simple .txt file in the folder.

It's inspired by the concept of a commonplace book, the notebooks writers and thinkers used to keep track of interesting passages, quotes, scriptures, scraps of thought, ephemera. Commonplace books were a precursor to modern curation and digital scrapbook culture.
